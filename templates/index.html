Quick picks:

At 0 / 0 / 0 nothing is changed. Minus values darken, cool or mute; plus values lighten, warm or strengthen.

Recommended settings
Small mosaics (3x3 to 5x5 plate units)

Dithering Soft, Grading Auto, Fewest colours, Skin protection on, Face boost off. Zoom in so the face fills most of the frame.

Large mosaics (6 or more plate units per side)

Dithering Soft, Grading Auto, Fewest colours, Skin protection on, Face boost off. For portraits, use a portrait size such as 6×8 or larger and frame the face prominently. Flags, logos and cartoons: leave Image type on automatic, they are detected and converted with the fewest possible colours and no mixing.
