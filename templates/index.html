Recommended settings
Small mosaics (1x1 to 2x2 plates)

Dithering Soft, Grading Auto, Skin protection on, Face boost off. Zoom in so the face fills most of the frame.

Large mosaics (3x3 plates and up)

Dithering Normal, Grading Auto, Skin protection on, Face boost off. Zoom out a little to include hair and shoulders.
